- Essential during the Research phase of the RPD loop
- I prompt with questions, not instructions
- Two advantages:
    - Verifies what the model "knows" internally and/or capable of discovering via web searches
    - Often is learning experience for me
    - It'll find information I didn't know to look for, or has been updated since I last learned
- Models are continually trained on the most recent best practices
- Even when their trained data is stale, it can find newer standards

## But don't take my word for it!

- What is the difference in your response and behavior when I ask a question vs give explicit instructions?
