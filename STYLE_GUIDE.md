# Blog Style Guide

## Voice

Write in first person. Use "I" and "me" rather than "you" or "we." Every post should draw from personal experience, not summarize or repackage someone else's content.

## Prose

Keep paragraphs short and direct. Cut filler words, qualifiers, and anything that doesn't move the point forward. If a sentence doesn't earn its place, remove it.

## Grammar

Maintain correct grammar and spelling throughout every post. Proofread before publishing.

## Post Structure

Every post must end with a section titled **"But don't take my word for it!"** containing:

1. A pre-written prompt the reader can paste into an AI chatbot to explore the topic on their own.
2. Links to any podcasts, blogs, or articles that informed the post.
